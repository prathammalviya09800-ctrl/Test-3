Five minutes on the flame

No premix, no machine. Each pot is made the same way, in this order.

  1. Crush fresh ginger and cardamom.
  2. Boil water with tea leaves until it turns dark.
  3. Add milk and sugar. Simmer until it rises twice.
  4. Pour from a height into the kulhad, so it cools a little and foams.
